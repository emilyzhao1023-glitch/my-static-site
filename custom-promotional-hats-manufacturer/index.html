Frequently Asked Questions

Bulk Promotional Hat FAQs

What is the MOQ for custom promotional hats?

The starting MOQ is 100 pieces. The final MOQ can depend on the hat style, color, material and decoration method. Volume planning is available for orders of 500 pieces and above.

Can you produce 1,000 or more hats for one campaign?

Yes. Send the total quantity, required delivery date, destination and number of designs. Production timing and packing requirements will be confirmed before the order.

Which logo methods are available?

Common options include flat embroidery, raised embroidery, woven or embroidered patches, rubber patches and printing. Availability depends on the selected hat and artwork.

What information is needed for a bulk quotation?

Please provide the hat style, quantity, logo artwork, decoration method if known, delivery country and required date. We can then confirm the specification and prepare pricing.

Can I approve a sample before bulk production?

Yes. A pre-production sample can be prepared for approval. Typical sample timing is 7 to 10 days and is confirmed after the design and material details are reviewed.

How long does bulk production take?

Typical bulk production is 15 to 30 days after sample approval and order confirmation. The final schedule depends on quantity, design complexity, materials and current capacity.

Can you support promotional product distributors?

Yes. Distributor projects can include artwork confirmation, production updates, neutral packing, carton marking and repeat-order specification records when agreed before production.

What shipping options are available?

Express, air and sea shipping options can be discussed according to the order size, destination and required delivery date. Freight is confirmed after the packed volume and destination are known.
